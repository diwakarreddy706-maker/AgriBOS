# AgriBOS

> Agricultural Business Operations System

AgriBOS is a full-stack agricultural business management system designed to manage agricultural machinery, farmers, inventory, billing, maintenance, operations, payments, and business finance from a centralized platform.

## 🚀 Features

- 🔐 User authentication and authorization
- 👨‍🌾 Farmer management
- 👥 Employee management
- 🚜 Agricultural machine management
- 📋 Machine booking and operations
- 🔧 Machine maintenance and breakdown tracking
- 📦 Inventory and spare-parts management
- ⛽ Fuel management
- 💰 Billing and invoicing
- 💳 Payment and cash-book management
- 📒 General ledger
- 📊 Profit and loss management
- 📈 Fleet and machine analytics
- 🤝 Machine-owner and rental settlement management
- 🧾 Vendor bills and payables
- 🚗 Vehicle compliance management
- 🌐 English and Kannada localization
- 📱 Responsive dashboard interface
